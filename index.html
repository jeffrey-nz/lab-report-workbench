It checks the sheet first

Every Avg, StDev and StErr the workbook states is recomputed from the animals above it. Disagreements, #VALUE! cells, rows marked don't use and inconsistent group labels are all reported before you plot anything.

The design picks the test

Animals measured more than once become a repeated-measures analysis; separate cohorts become an independent-groups analysis. It says which it chose and why, and gives you F(df, df), p and the multiple comparisons.

One figure, one file

Panels are drawn together and lettered A, B, C, with mean ± SEM, every animal shown and significance marked. Exports as a single 300 dpi PNG, or as vector SVG.

It drafts the words

A figure legend and a results paragraph carrying your real numbers, in the structure these are marked on. A starting point to rewrite — not something to submit.

What the workbook actually contains

Everything below is recomputed from the individual animal values. Where the workbook's own Avg / StDev / StErr cells disagree with the animals above them, that is flagged here rather than carried into your figures.
